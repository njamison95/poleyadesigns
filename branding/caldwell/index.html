Educating Caldwell Road Poleya Designs George Mason University

Inclusivity. Care. Empowerment.

Educating Caldwell Road is a school in Liberia founded by Elizabeth Anderson Bowen that aims to empower its students through community-oriented education.


As a new school, it needed a visual identity that reflected its values and distinct approach to teaching. The visual identity features bright, bold colors and a bird logo turned backward to symbolize the founder giving back to her community.

Primary Font

Besley

Black

Besley is a slab serif font designed by Owen Earl. It is named after Robert Besley, the creater of the typeface Clarendon.

The logo for ERC uses the typeface Besley in the format Black, which is even bolder than Bold. Besley is a slab serif typeface that combines approachability with trustworthiness, effectively encapsulating the core values of ECR as a primary school.

Secondary Font

Inter

Regular

Inter is a typeface created by Rasmus Andersson specifically for computer screens, although it can easily be applied on print materials as well. It is a tried and true style, used worldwide by big companies like Pixar and Mozilla.

Inter’s sans serif quality provides a pleasing contrast to Gelo’s thick serifs. It balances professional legibility with calmness. It also has a wide font family ranging from Thin (the thinnest variation) to Black (the thickest), making it extremely versatile.
