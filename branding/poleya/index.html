Empathy. Independence. Grit.

Poleya Designs is the personal brand I created as an authentic expression of who I am both as a designer and a person. As a designer, I strive to create cohesive, strategic visuals that tell my clients' story and embody their values and mission.


Rooted in empathy and resilience, it embodies my values and creative evolution. I chose the moth as my logo to represent perserverence, transformation, and the pursuit of light even in the darkest moments. It is a reminder that the journey holds as much meaning as the destination.

Primary Font

Rubik

Extra Bold

Rubik is a slightly-rounded sans-serif typeface designed by Philipp Hubert and Sebastian Fischer at Hubert & Fischer. It's development was part of Chrome Cube Lab, a project in which the classic Rubik's cube was reimagined in various experiments on the web.

The Poleya Designs logo uses Rubik in Extra Bold to symbolize my strive to create bold, impactful designs. With a historical connection to the Rubik's cube, it also represents the creative problem-solving I undertake with each design.

Secondary Font

Gantari

Regular

Gantari is a sans-serif typeface designed by Anugrah Pasau from the type foundry Lafontype. It is almost geometric, though the proportions were adjusted slightly to create a more balanced look.

I chose to use Gantari Regular as a secondary font because the stroke shapes and counters reflect those found in the Poleya Designs moth graphic.
