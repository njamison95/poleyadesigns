Educating Caldwell Road Poleya Designs George Mason University

Social media graphics and videos for George Mason University's College of Science.


George Mason University underwent a rebrand in the summer of 2024. I had already been working in the old brand for a year.


Participating in this rebrand challenged me to develop a deeper understanding for how brand identities work, why they exist, and how to design creatively within them.
